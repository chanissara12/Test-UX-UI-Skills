Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The 14-day trial and the Starter plan need only an email address.

Can I import from other tools?

Yes. One-click importers cover Notion, Trello, Asana, Jira and CSV files.

How does billing work for growing teams?

You pay per active person, prorated daily. Add or remove seats anytime.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we are SOC 2 Type II certified.
